FAQ

Common Questions

How quickly can you start an engagement?

For consulting engagements, we typically have consultants available within 1-2 weeks. Full implementations go through a brief scoping phase (1-2 weeks) before kickoff. In urgent situations, we can fast-track.

Are your consultants Workday-certified?

Yes, every consultant holds at minimum one Workday Pro certification, with most holding 3+. We are a Workday Sales & Services Partner with access to the latest certification programs.

Can you work alongside our internal team or other partners?

Absolutely. Many of our engagements are collaborative — we augment your existing team or work alongside other Workday partners. We're flexible on governance models and communication structures.

What does the Tenant Health Check include?

We review your security model, business process configurations, integration architecture, custom reports, and feature utilization against Workday best practices. You get a prioritized improvement plan with estimated effort and impact.
