📐 Page Architecture Decision

Structure: One services hub page + 3 dedicated service pages (Deploy, Consult, Elevate)

Why not tabs: Tabbed content = 1 URL = all three services share one page's SEO equity. Separate pages let each service rank independently for its own keyword cluster.

URL structure:

/services/ → Hub page (this wireframe) — targets "workday consulting services"

/services/workday-implementation/ → Deploy — targets "workday implementation partner UK"

/services/workday-consulting/ → Consult — targets "workday consulting services"

/services/workday-optimization/ → Elevate — targets "workday optimization services"

Audit context: Current site uses blog-post redirects for services. No proper landing pages exist. This is the #1 structural fix.
